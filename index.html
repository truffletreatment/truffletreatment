Psilocybin · Guided Sessions

Guided psilocybin
sessions

A safe and supportive space for self-exploration, emotional healing, and deeper connection.

Get in Touch

Psilocybin-containing mushrooms and truffles have been used ceremonially by indigenous cultures for generations as tools for healing, insight, and connection.

These experiences can create space for deeper self-awareness, emotional openness, and new perspectives.

Psilocybin has the potential to help you break free from entrenched beliefs and thought patterns that may be holding you back or causing distress. It can enable the mind to explore new perspectives and possibilities.

Research suggests that psilocybin may temporarily increase neuroplasticity, allowing the brain to become more adaptable and receptive to new perspectives, behaviours, and emotional processing.

The importance of preparation

The quality of preparation before a psychedelic experience has a direct correlation to the benefits individuals receive from the session. Adequate preparation can contribute to a more positive and transformative experience.

Studies have shown that individuals who engage in thorough preparation tend to report greater psychological insights, emotional healing, and long-term positive changes. This underscores the importance of mindful and intentional preparation as a crucial aspect of maximising the therapeutic potential of psychedelics.

Here are some suggestions designed to help you create the optimal mindset for the journey ahead:

Nourish Your Body

Lean toward whole, nourishing foods — fresh vegetables, fruits, whole grains, and quality protein. Reducing excess sugar and processed foods helps you feel more balanced and present.

It's recommended to avoid alcohol and recreational substances for at least one week before the session.

Calm Your Mind

Engage in mindfulness practices such as yoga, meditation, journaling, creative art, connecting with nature, and breathwork to centre your mind.

Digital Detox

Minimise social media usage, news consumption, and screen time in the days leading to the ceremony. Create a serene mental space by avoiding external influences that may impact your state of mind.

Set Your Intentions

Take some time to reflect on your intentions for the ceremony. How would you like to approach the experience? How can you best support yourself leading up to the ceremony?

Practise Self-care

Preparation is an opportunity to reflect on your habits, routines, and emotional well-being. What supports your sense of balance and clarity? Where might you benefit from slowing down or creating more space for yourself?

How does your psilocybin journey look?

After our initial conversation and screening process, follows your preparation time. At the day of the session, I will come to a location of your choice — a place that brings you a sense of comfort and instinctual safety.

Phase 01

In your safe space

After arriving, I will slowly prepare the space to create a calm and supportive atmosphere. We will find a spot for you where most of the experience will take place — most likely a couch, or somewhere equally comfortable.

Phase 02

Preparing the medicine

I will have prepared the truffles beforehand and once I am at yours, I will add them to a fresh ginger tea. That way it is easier for your body to digest the truffles and get the psilocybin working soon.

After a short meditation and opening the sacred space, you will slowly begin to drink the medicine while sharing your intentions for the ceremony.

Phase 03

The journey itself

The truffles will begin to take effect about 20–60 minutes after consumption. You may begin to notice shifts in perception, emotions, body sensations, and visual awareness.

Once you are ready, I will invite you to lay in your comfortable space with your eyes covered, and journey inwards. I will sit back and hold space for you while the music guides you. The experience unfolds naturally while I remain present to support and guide you when needed.

Phase 04

Recover, relax, reflect

After about 4 hours, you'll start coming down from the journey — smoothly and gradually. There will be enough time to share how you feel or simply sit in silence while landing.

Once I make sure you are safe on your own I will leave, and you are free to spend the evening however you like. You will be hungry, and you will have a lot to process, so it is my recommendation not to plan anything for that evening. Perhaps journal, perhaps just relax, perhaps just eat. It is up to you.

The journey continues...

Being able to integrate the insights of our psychedelic experience is essential to the healing process.

In the days and weeks following the session, many people notice increased openness, emotional clarity, and a greater capacity for change and reflection. This period of heightened neuroplasticity offers a unique opportunity to integrate insights gained during the psychedelic experience into daily life, ultimately promoting personal growth and well-being.

Some tips on how to stay connected to the energy of the ceremony afterwards: talk about your experience with people who are open to listen, support and understand you; meditate; journal; spend some time in nature reflecting on your newfound sense of being; discover new ways to connect with and express your creativity.

We will be in touch for an integration call and any other way I can support you throughout this process.

In order to evaluate your mental and physical health and well-being to ascertain the suitability of the experience for you, please take the time to fill out the screening form prior to our intake.

About Me

Hi,
I'm Polina.

My journey as a psychedelic facilitator started in 2016 as a natural continuation of my personal path.

I received an invitation from a friend to participate in a plant medicine ceremony, an event that turned out to be the most profound and life-altering experience I've ever had. It marked a pivotal moment in my life, guiding me toward a completely new path — one of deep inner exploration and transformation.

I see life through a holistic view and I believe that achieving and maintaining good health asks from us to look for alternative and ancient ways to restore balance in our minds and bodies. Becoming a Level 3 Breathcoach through the training program of Kasper van der Meulen (breathworkmasterclass.com) has inspired me to dive deep into the biology and biochemistry of the body and implement breathwork as a very powerful tool to release stress and tension from our system, return to a balanced state, and find a deeper understanding of our bodies.

In my work, I incorporate breathwork, sound, movement, bodywork, somatic experiencing techniques, meditation and emotional release, that help facilitate the deep transformative aspects of the journeys I offer.

All the years of experience have provided me with valuable insights into the crucial significance of having the proper support and care during our personal journeys towards wholeness. Considering the humbling, profound and introspective nature of the sessions that I offer, through a screening process, I make sure that you are ready and eligible to take on that journey.

My intention is to create an environment where you feel safe, acknowledged, heard and truly understood — recognising these as essential elements in the healing process.

I hold deep gratitude for every chance I've had to guide individuals through the remarkable and transformative journeys that magic truffles and breathwork can offer.

I believe in the inherent potential within each one of us to live a life enriched by freedom, happiness and a profound love for existence — and healing ourselves is essential to inviting and embodying that potential.
